The Grocery Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how The Grocery collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our mobile application and related delivery services.

Service: The Grocery
Service Area: Sahiwal, Punjab, Pakistan
Last Updated: September 26, 2026
Privacy Contact: grocery.pk.store@gmail.com

1. Overview & Service Scope

The Grocery ("we", "our", or "us") provides a localized grocery ordering and doorstep delivery application designed to serve customers located in Sahiwal, Punjab, Pakistan.

We respect your personal privacy and are committed to transparency in our data processing activities. This policy applies to all registered users and customers placing orders through the The Grocery Android mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the categories of data strictly required to establish your customer account, verify your identity, process grocery orders, and fulfill deliveries to your designated address in Sahiwal.

A. Personal Information You Provide Directly

  • Full Name: Collected during account registration, profile setup, and order checkout. We use your name to identify your account, communicate with you regarding your orders, and address your delivery packages.
  • Mobile Phone Number: Your verified Pakistani mobile number (+923XXXXXXXXX) serves as your primary account identifier. It is used to deliver verification codes via WhatsApp, send critical order notifications, and enable delivery personnel to contact you during order handoff.
  • Delivery Addresses: Details including house number, street name, colony or area name, landmark, and optional delivery instructions in Sahiwal. This information is saved to your account profile so you can conveniently select delivery destinations during checkout.
  • Optional Profile Image: If you choose to set an avatar or profile image, the image file is stored securely in our cloud storage associated with your user identifier.

B. Order & Transaction Information

  • Order History & Details: Information regarding the items you order, item quantities, product pricing, subtotal, delivery fee, order status history (placed, confirmed, packing, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled), delivery time round estimates, and order creation timestamps.
  • Favorite Products: When you mark products as favorites within the app, the list of favorite product identifiers is saved under your account profile in our database so you can easily re-order them.

C. Technical, Device & Security Data

  • Notification Tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging): A device registration token assigned by Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) is stored with your user profile to send real-time push notifications regarding order status updates (such as confirmation, packing, dispatch, and delivery).
  • App Integrity & Security Data (Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity): When interacting with our backend services, your device transmits cryptographic integrity attestations via Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check. This verifies that requests originate from an authentic, unmodified The Grocery application on a certified Android device, protecting our service against bots, scrapers, and fraudulent activity.

What We Do NOT Collect:

  • No Payment Card Data: The Grocery operates on a Cash on Delivery (COD) payment model. We do NOT collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking credentials.
  • No Background GPS Location Tracking: We do NOT collect or track your continuous physical GPS location in the background. Address information is entered directly by you.
  • No Advertising Trackers or Data Brokers: We do NOT sell your data, use third-party behavioral advertising networks, or embed tracking cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

The Grocery uses your personal data solely for legitimate operational and service delivery purposes:

  • Account Authentication & Management: To verify your mobile number via WhatsApp OTP, manage your customer account, and maintain your login session securely without passwords.
  • Order Processing & Delivery: To receive grocery orders, verify product pricing and availability, pack ordered items, calculate delivery windows, and dispatch orders to your physical address in Sahiwal.
  • Order Status Alerts & Communications: To send real-time push notifications and transactional messages regarding order updates, dispatch status, or delays.
  • Customer Support: To assist you with order inquiries, delivery coordination, missing item resolutions, and account management requests.
  • Service Security & Fraud Prevention: To enforce rate limits, detect and prevent bot spam or fake orders, protect backend APIs, and safeguard system integrity.
  • Operational Analytics & Service Improvement: To monitor inventory needs, ensure reliable delivery rounds, and optimize grocery availability in Sahiwal.

5. Payment Model: Cash on Delivery (COD)

All orders placed on The Grocery are fulfilled on a Cash on Delivery (COD) basis. Customers pay the delivery rider directly in cash upon receiving and verifying their grocery package at their doorstep in Sahiwal.

Because all transactions are settled via Cash on Delivery, our mobile application does not incorporate online payment gateways, does not collect payment-card numbers, and does not retain financial banking information.

6. Data Security Practices

We employ technical and administrative security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration:

  • Transport Encryption: All data exchanged between your mobile device and our backend infrastructure is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS cryptographic protocols.
  • Secure OTP Hashing: One-time password verification challenges are hashed using HMAC-SHA256 with a secret pepper on our backend servers. Plaintext verification codes are never stored in our database.
  • Rate Limiting & Anti-Brute-Force: OTP verification requests and order submissions are governed by strict transaction-safe rate limits (cooldown intervals, hourly caps, and daily send limits) to protect against automated abuse.
  • Granular Access Controls: Database security rules and backend validation logic enforce strict isolation, ensuring customers can only access and modify their own personal profile, addresses, and order history.
  • Backend Secret Isolation: Service provider API credentials, database keys, and configuration secrets are maintained exclusively in secure server-side environments and are never bundled in the mobile application binary.

Please note: While we implement robust, industry-standard security safeguards to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission over the internet or method of digital storage can be guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, maintain active customer accounts, and satisfy operational, legal, and accounting obligations.

Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your The Grocery account at any time. When an account deletion is initiated:

  • Delivery Addresses: All saved delivery addresses stored in your personal subcollection are permanently and immediately deleted from the database.
  • Personal Profile Anonymization: Your profile document is permanently stripped of identifying information: your full name is overwritten to "Deleted User", phone number to "Deleted", and email, FCM notification tokens, profile image URLs, and notification preferences are cleared (set to null). The account is marked inactive.
  • Authentication Removal: Your account record in Firebase Authentication is permanently deleted, preventing any future sign-in with your previous credentials.
  • Historical Order Records: When you delete your account, The Grocery anonymizes all personal identifiers in your order history. Fields including your Firebase user ID, name, phone number, full delivery address, and delivery instructions are overwritten with neutral placeholder values. The non-personal transaction record (items, quantities, prices, order number, payment method, status history, and timestamps) is retained for legitimate business, accounting, and operational purposes. Retained order documents are marked with a customerDeleted flag to indicate that the associated customer account has been removed.

For detailed instructions on deleting your account inside the app or submitting a request via email, please visit our Account Deletion Guide.

8. Children's Privacy

The Grocery is intended for a general audience capable of entering into binding delivery and purchase agreements. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from individuals under the age of 13.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that information from our records. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately at grocery.pk.store@gmail.com.

  • Push Notification Preferences: You can manage or disable push notification permissions at any time through your Android device system settings.
  • Account Deletion: You may permanently delete your account directly through the mobile app or by sending an email request to our support team.
