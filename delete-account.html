Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your GroceryGo Account

We respect your right to control your personal data. Below you will find complete, step-by-step instructions for permanently deleting your GroceryGo account and associated personal data, both within the mobile app and via email request.

Service: GroceryGo
Jurisdiction: Sahiwal, Punjab, Pakistan
Direct Support: grocery.pk.store@gmail.com

Important: Account Deletion is Permanent
Deleting your GroceryGo account cannot be undone. Once deleted, you will lose access to your profile, saved delivery addresses, and favorite products. Any active login sessions will be immediately terminated.

Method 1: In the GroceryGo Mobile App (Immediate)

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the Android application at any time. This process is instant and automated:

1
Open the App & Sign In Ensure you are signed in to the GroceryGo app with the phone number you wish to delete.
2
Navigate to the Profile Screen Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Locate the "Danger Zone" Section Scroll down toward the bottom of the Profile screen to find the section clearly titled Danger Zone.
4
Tap "Delete Account" Tap the Delete Account option highlighted in red.
5
Confirm Permanent Deletion A confirmation dialog titled "Delete Account?" will appear asking: "Are you sure you want to permanently delete your account and all associated data? This action cannot be undone."
Tap Delete to confirm.

Upon confirmation, the app executes our secure backend deletion function, clears all local credentials, and automatically logs you out.

Method 2: Outside the App via Email Request

If you have uninstalled the GroceryGo app, no longer have access to your mobile device, or prefer to request deletion externally, you can submit an account deletion request directly to our privacy support team.

Send an email to:
grocery.pk.store@gmail.com

Information to Include in Your Email:

  • Registered Mobile Phone Number: Provide the exact Pakistani mobile number used when creating your GroceryGo account (e.g., 03XXXXXXXXX or +923XXXXXXXXX).
  • Account Full Name: Provide the full name registered on your GroceryGo profile.

Security Notice — What NOT to Send:

For your security, NEVER send passwords, OTP verification codes, payment card numbers, bank details, or Firebase internal credentials in your email. Our team will verify your request using only your registered mobile number and profile name.

Our support team reviews account deletion requests manually and processes them through our backend deletion procedure once the identity of the account holder is verified.

What Data is Deleted, Anonymized, or Retained

To maintain complete transparency, the table below outlines exactly how our backend handles each category of data when an account is deleted:

Data Category Action Taken Details & Reason
Saved Delivery Addresses Permanently Deleted All saved delivery addresses in your account subcollection are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our database immediately.
Authentication Credentials Permanently Deleted Your user record is deleted from Firebase Authentication. You can no longer authenticate or sign in using that identity.
Profile Identifiers & Preferences Anonymized Your profile document is anonymized: full name is set to "Deleted User", phone number is set to "Deleted", and email, push notification tokens (FCM), profile photo URL, and notification preferences are cleared (set to null). The account is marked inactive.
Historical Order Records Retained (Anonymized) All personal identifiers in past orders are anonymized: your account UID, name, phone number, full delivery address, and delivery instructions are overwritten with neutral placeholder values at the time of account deletion. The non-personal transaction record (items, quantities, prices, subtotals, fees, order number, payment method, order status, and timestamps) is retained for accounting, fulfillment auditing, and fraud prevention purposes.
