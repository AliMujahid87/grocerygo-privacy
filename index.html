App & Privacy Summary

GroceryGo provides fast, reliable grocery delivery within Sahiwal. We adhere strictly to data minimization and user safety principles:

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Only: GroceryGo operates exclusively on Cash on Delivery. We do not collect, process, or store credit card, debit card, bank account, or CVV information.
  • Passwordless WhatsApp Authentication: You log in securely using 6-digit one-time verification codes (OTPs) sent directly to your verified Pakistani mobile number via WhatsApp. No account passwords to remember or compromise.
  • Delivery Focused Data: We only collect information strictly required to pack and deliver your groceries to your doorstep (name, phone number, delivery address in Sahiwal, and order details).
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not track your activity across third-party websites or sell your personal information to advertising networks.
  • Permanent Deletion on Request: You can delete your account and personal identifiers directly in the mobile app at any time, or contact our support team to request deletion.
